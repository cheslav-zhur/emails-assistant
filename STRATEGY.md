# emails-assistant Strategy

## Purpose

Sort my own mail: what to delete, how to group it, including from the IDE. And get a daily summary automatically, save it locally, and decide later where it goes.

Worth revisiting: what makes this hard right now was never named.

## Positioning

The mailboxes are mine. Get extracts from them and feed those into my AI system, so sorting and the daily summary do not live in someone else's mail client. Message text may go to an outside model in the cloud. For now the summary is written to a file in the repo.

## Users

**Primary:** me, the owner of these mailboxes. In the morning I'm hiring emails-assistant to give me a mail summary quickly. At other times, to put the mail in order: what to delete, how to group it.

## Boundaries

- Delete or group messages in Gmail without my decision.
- Remove the file in the repo and send the summary straight to the AI system. Feeding the extract to the AI system later is fine; doing that instead of the file is not, until I say otherwise.
- Write reply drafts. Not for now.

_Resist a change when:_ it deletes or groups messages without my decision, removes the repo file and leaves only sending the summary to the AI system, or writes reply drafts.

- **I read the summary and make decisions.** Where this is recorded was not said.
- **Order in the mailbox improves.** How to check it, and where, was not said.
